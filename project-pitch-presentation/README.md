# Project Pitch Presentation

**Points: 6**


#### Objectives:
- Present the project in the form of a business pitch aimed at engaging potential investors.  
- Highlight the key advantages of the project, the problems it addresses, and its potential business benefits.  

#### Presentation Format:
- **Duration**: Maximum 3 minutes.  
- **Format**: The presentation can be conducted live or recorded (video, multimedia presentation with voice narration).  
- **Target Audience**: Potential investors or clients interested in implementing the solution.  

#### Focus Areas (timings are approximate):  

**Problem and Need (30 seconds)**  
- Describe the problem your project solves and why it is significant.  
- Provide numerical data or other evidence demonstrating the scale of the problem.  

**Solution (30 seconds)**  
- Present your solution clearly and concisely.  
- Highlight what makes your project stand out from others.  

**Technology and Innovation (30 seconds)**  
- Explain the technology behind your project (e.g., machine learning, task automation).  
- Identify the key technical components that make the project effective.  

**Results and Business Value (45 seconds)**  
- Showcase the outcomes of your project, e.g., model accuracy, time savings, scalability.  
- Explain the business benefits that can result from its implementation.  

**Future Plans and Growth Potential (45 seconds)**  
- Present your plans for the project's further development.  
- Identify potential markets for expansion and how the project could evolve.  

**Conclusion (30 seconds)**  
- Summarize the key points of the presentation.  
- End with a strong “call to action” (e.g., “Invest in our project to solve problem X together and capitalize on the growing Y market”).  

#### Task Deliverables:  
- **Link** to the recorded presentation or confirmation of its live delivery.  
- **Multimedia presentation** (PDF, PowerPoint, or another format) supporting your speech.  
- **Final project report**, including:  
  - Summary of all project stages.  
  - Description of challenges encountered and how they were addressed.  
  - Conclusions and reflections on the project process.  

#### Scoring (6 points):  

1. **Clarity and Structure of Presentation (3 points)**  
   - Is the presentation understandable and well-organized?  

2. **Inclusion of Key Elements (3 points)**  
   - Are all required elements (problem, solution, technology, results, future) covered?  

3. **Professionalism and Engagement (2 points)**  
   - Is the presentation visually appealing and engaging?  
   - Was the speaking style convincing and professional?  

4. **Final Report (2 points)**  
   - Does the report effectively summarize the project and include all required elements?  

Good luck with creating a professional and inspiring business presentation!
